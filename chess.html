How to Play?

  1. White moves first, then turns alternate.
  2. Click one of your pieces to select it. Its legal moves light up.
  3. Click a highlighted square to move there or capture.
  4. You can't leave your own king in check. Pawns promote to a queen.

Castling and en passant are not included.
